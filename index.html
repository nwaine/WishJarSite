How it works

You’re in charge. Kids can only ask.

Every point is earned through a task you approved. Balances are calculated, never edited — so there’s nothing to argue about.

1

Set up tasks

Daily, certain days, once a week, or one-time. Give each one the points it’s worth.

2

Kids check them off

On their own compatible iPhone or the family iPad. WishJar requires iOS 26.1 or iPadOS 26.1 or later, so check an older device before handing it down. No account, no password, just a six-letter family code.

3

You approve in one tap

From the app, the Lock Screen, or your Apple Watch. Add a friendly note when it helps.

4

Points become rewards

Rewards you chose, at the cost you set. Kids redeem when they can afford it.

What makes it WishJar

They wish. You decide.

✨

Wishes and goals

Kids can wish for something — “Sleepover at Grandma’s.” You grant it as a reward, turn a big one into a long-term goal, or say not this time. Goals measure effort, not savings, so spending points on small treats never sets the big thing back.

👨‍👩‍👧

Any grown-ups you choose

Works solo, or invite a co-parent, a grandparent, or any grown-up who helps out. Every grown-up can approve and gets notified. Joining needs an existing grown-up’s okay — a family code alone is never enough.

⌚️

Approve from your wrist

Task alerts land on your Apple Watch and the Lock Screen, so “Riley finished Make bed” is a tap away wherever you are.

🔒

Private by design

No ads, no analytics, no tracking. Kids never create accounts and can’t enter personal information. Delete a kid’s device or your entire account right from the app.

Screens

The parent dashboard, the kid’s screen, and everything between.
