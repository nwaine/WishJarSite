Common questions

How does a kid join?

On the kid’s device, open WishJar and tap I’m a kid — enter a join code, type your family’s six-letter code (Home → house icon), and pick their name. No account or password. If you ever want a new code, tap Get a new code — kids already signed in stay signed in.

A kid marked a task done but the points didn’t change.

That’s by design: points are awarded when a grown-up approves. Check Needs You on your dashboard, or the notification on your phone or watch, and tap Approve.

Can another parent or a grandparent help?

Yes. They sign up as a grown-up and ask to join with your family code; you approve the request from your dashboard. Every grown-up can approve tasks and gets notified. A code alone is never enough to get in.

What’s the difference between a reward and a goal?

A reward is something a kid can buy with points right now. A goal is a big wish you’ve turned into something to work toward over time — it fills up from the same effort, but spending points on small rewards never sets it back.

How do I manage or cancel the subscription?

On your iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → WishJar. One subscription covers the whole family. If you started a free trial, cancel at least 24 hours before it ends to avoid a charge. Otherwise, cancel at least 24 hours before the current subscription period ends to stop renewal.

How do I remove a kid’s device, or delete everything?

Open the kid’s detail screen → Devices → Unlink. To delete your whole account: Home → house icon → Delete account. See the Privacy Policy for exactly what that erases.
